# Hello, World: presenter notes

These notes appear on your phone or in the presenter window (press P). Each section matches a slide by its ID in brackets.

## [hello] Hello, world

- Let the particles finish spelling "Hello" before you speak.
- This is a Cue cartridge: a single HTML file, with no dependencies.
- Press the right arrow, a clicker, or Next on your phone to continue.

## [cartridge] What a cartridge is

- A cartridge is just a web page with a few conventions.
- **Click 1:** Slides. Each section is a slide with a permanent ID, so these notes never drift when you reorder.
- **Click 2:** Builds. Anything with data-b appears on a click. This card is one.
- **Click 3:** Notes. This text lives in notes.md, and the remote highlights the next click for you.

## [controls] Drive it from anywhere

- **Click 1:** Arrows, Page Up and Page Down, or any clicker.
- **Click 2:** Q shows the QR code to pair a phone. P opens this view on the same computer.
- **Click 3:** B blacks out the screen when you want the room's attention. F goes full screen.
- Try it now: press B, then B again.

## [your-turn] Now make your own

- **Click 1:** Copy this folder into your slides repository and rename it.
- **Click 2:** Give your agent the starter prompt from docs/SETUP.md, plus your topic.
- **Click 3:** Push. The library updates on its own.
- **Click 4:** Go build something beautiful.
